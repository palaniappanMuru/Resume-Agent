Role Overview 

We are seeking a Senior Engineering Manager to lead the engineering organization for Tekion Automotive Marketing Cloud (TAMC) — a fully AI-native product built to solve the marketing and advertising needs of automotive industry partners. TAMC is one of Tekion’s most strategic new product lines, and this role is central to its success.
You will be hands-on from day one — directly owning sprint cadence, technical direction, and individual engineer development. As we launch and scale, Engineering Managers will be hired into the structure, evolving this into a broader leadership role. You will also be the primary engineering counterpart to product and business partners across India and US time zones, ensuring tight alignment between business goals and engineering execution


Key Responsibilities

Directly lead the TAMC engineering team (~20 engineers): two Scrum teams, Staff/Senior engineers, Applied ML, and Staff SDET — with the expectation of building out a management layer as the org scales.
Own delivery accountability: sprint cadence, cross-team dependency management, risk tracking, and executive reporting on engineering progress.
Set and enforce technical standards: hexagonal architecture discipline, AI-native integration patterns, code quality gates, and observability standards across both teams.
Act as the primary engineering counterpart to product and business partners across India and US time zones — translate business requirements into engineering execution plans, surface trade-offs early, and maintain shared understanding of what is being built and why.
Own the cross-team coordination interface to platform teams (Agentic Engine, Core Platform, ARC backend, Design) — ensuring TAMC’s dependencies are tracked, escalated, and resolved on schedule.
Hire, develop, and retain top engineering talent: own the full hiring pipeline, define career ladders, conduct performance reviews, and make compensation decisions.
Drive the technical roadmap for the next wave of AI capabilities in partnership with the ML Architect, Applied Scientist, and Product leadership.
Maintain technical depth: actively participate in architecture reviews, challenge design decisions, and unblock engineers on complex distributed systems and AI integration challenges.
Build and sustain a high-trust, high-accountability engineering culture where engineers feel ownership, psychological safety, and pride in their craft
