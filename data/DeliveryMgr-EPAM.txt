About the job
We are seeking a highly experienced Senior Delivery Manager to lead the delivery and transformation of a mission-critical business application within a complex, distributed and regulated environment. This role is pivotal in driving application development in a brownfield environment, ensuring seamless integration, modernization and operational excellence. The ideal candidate brings deep experience in insurance or reinsurance, exceptional leadership in Agile project management and proven ability to run Agile teams and manage Agile Scrum ceremonies.
 
Responsibilities

Lead end-to-end delivery of complex business applications, ensuring quality, timelines and business outcomes
Manage small to medium-scale development initiatives in brownfield environments, including modernization and transformation efforts
Oversight of multi-disciplinary teams across distributed locations, spanning engineering, QA, architecture, DevOps and BA
Define and enforce delivery governance, risk management and compliance frameworks
Champion Agile practices such as Scrum, driving continuous improvement and predictable delivery
Facilitation of sprint planning, backlog refinement, release planning and cross-team coordination
Track delivery metrics and proactively identify risks, dependencies and impediments
Act as the primary delivery interface for business, technology and executive stakeholders
Translate business objectives into actionable plans and technical roadmaps
Provide guidance on application development, modernization and integration, while collaborating closely with the solution architect to define scalable, resilient and secure system designs
Ensure adherence to regulatory requirements relevant to insurance and reinsurance through audit-compliant delivery processes
Oversight of Run and Build operations across highly distributed, high-availability environments, while driving continuous service improvement and automation to reduce operational risk
 
Requirements

16-20 years of experience in software development, with 3-4 years in a delivery or project manager role
Knowledge of Java-based applications, Azure cloud services and Angular
Proficiency in databases such as MS SQL Server and PostgreSQL
Proven delivery experience in complex, distributed and regulated environments
Background in managing brownfield application development and transformation
Strong background in the insurance or reinsurance domain
Expertise in Agile methodologies and enterprise delivery frameworks
Excellent stakeholder communication, negotiation and leadership skills
Familiarity with DevOps pipelines, CI/CD and automation frameworks
Experience working with global teams in onsite-offshore models
Mandatory use of AI tools for at least the last 4 projects
 
Nice to have

ITIL v4 Foundation or higher certification
PMP, PRINCE2, or equivalent project management certification
Flexibility to use cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure and GCP alongside DevOps practices
Understanding of regulatory and compliance requirements in the insurance and reinsurance sector
