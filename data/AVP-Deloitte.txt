The Product Engineering Manager is a key leadership role in product team primarily responsible for building and delivering the enterprise software product that is implemented for multiple clients. Development/Engineering manager is a hands-on technical manager managing significant strategic and technical aspect of products, including communications with Leadership, leading multifunctional teams, quality, and adherence to Deloitte standards and processes.

Leadership & Delivery

Build and lead a high-performing engineering organization (engineers and engineering managers), including hiring, coaching, and performance management.
